Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Riwood Entertainment ("we") handles information in the Riwood Entertainment Pipeline, our internal software for producing and uploading our own videos.

Who uses the pipeline

The pipeline is used only by Riwood Entertainment to manage our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not sign in or collect data from other people.

YouTube API Services

The pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and our use is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

With our permission, the pipeline accesses our own YouTube account in two ways:

  • Upload videos to our channel (always as private first).
  • Read the status of our own videos, for example to confirm a new upload is private.

It does not read, store or share data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

What we store

  • The access credentials Google issues for our own account, kept in encrypted secret storage and never published.
  • Our own production records, such as scripts, video files, upload IDs and costs.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through YouTube API Services, and we do not use it for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Stored credentials are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
